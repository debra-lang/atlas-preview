About this profile

Your answers are stored only in this browser (nothing is uploaded), and you can export or delete them at any time. The guidance-check above is based on published clinical practice guidelines — the AAO-HNSF Clinical Practice Guideline: Tinnitus (2014) and the AAO-HNSF Clinical Practice Guideline: Sudden Hearing Loss (2019) — which identify characteristics that warrant professional evaluation. This page cannot diagnose the cause of tinnitus and does not replace an ENT physician or audiologist. We deliberately use plain questions rather than licensed clinical questionnaires (THI/TFI), which have usage restrictions and belong in clinical care.
