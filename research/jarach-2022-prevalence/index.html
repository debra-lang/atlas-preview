Global prevalence and incidence of tinnitus: systematic review and meta-analysis

Record details

  • Authors: Jarach CM, et al.
  • Journal / source: JAMA Neurology (2022)
  • Design: Systematic review + meta-analysis (epidemiology — context record, not treatment evidence)
  • Sample: 83 prevalence articles pooled
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

Pooled estimates: about 14% of adults report any tinnitus (≈2% severe); prevalence rises with age and is broadly similar across sexes. The standard modern citation for how common tinnitus is.

Estimates vary with definitions and instruments across the underlying studies.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Heterogeneous definitions across included studies.

Funding / conflicts: Independent.
