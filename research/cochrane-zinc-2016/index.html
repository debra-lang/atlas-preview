Zinc supplementation for tinnitus (Cochrane Review)

Record details

  • Authors: Person OC, et al.
  • Journal / source: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews (2016)
  • Design: Systematic review
  • Sample: 209 (3 studies)
  • Primary endpoint: Tinnitus symptoms
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

No evidence that oral zinc improves tinnitus symptoms in adults.
