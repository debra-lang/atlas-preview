Cochrane: anticonvulsants for tinnitus (CD007960)

Record details

  • Authors: Hoekstra CE, et al.
  • Journal / source: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews (2011)
  • Design: Cochrane systematic review
  • Sample: 7 RCTs, 453 patients
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

No evidence of a large positive effect; a small effect 'of doubtful clinical significance' was demonstrated, with significant risk of bias.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Underlying trials at significant risk of bias.

Funding / conflicts: Independent.
