Internet-based audiologist-guided CBT for tinnitus: US randomized controlled trial

Record details

  • Authors: Beukes EW, et al.
  • Journal / source: Journal of Medical Internet Research (2022)
  • Design: RCT
  • Sample: 158 (79 iCBT / 79 monitoring)
  • Primary endpoint: TFI
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

Post-treatment TFI 36.6 vs 46.3 (Cohen d = 0.46). Clinically significant improvement in 57% vs 15% — by the paper's own reliable-change criterion, not the standard 13-point TFI threshold.

Control was weekly monitoring (no active comparator). 1-year follow-up paper reported maintained efficacy (PMID 36353148).

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Self-selected online sample; unblinded.

Funding / conflicts: Academic.
