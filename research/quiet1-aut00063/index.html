QUIET-1: AUT00063 (Kv3 modulator) Phase 2a — terminated for futility

Record details

  • Authors: Hall DA, Ray J, Watson J, et al.
  • Journal / source: Hearing Research (2019)
  • Design: Randomized placebo-controlled Phase 2a
  • Sample: 91
  • Primary endpoint: TFI
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: No

What this research found

Terminated early for futility; TFI change ~1.6 points despite therapeutic-range drug exposure.
