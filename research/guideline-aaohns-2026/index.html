AAO-HNSF Clinical Practice Guideline: Tinnitus Update (2026)

Record details

  • Authors: Adams ME, Cramer JD, Tunkel DE, et al. (21 authors)
  • Journal / source: Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery (2026)
  • Design: Clinical practice guideline (US), update of the 2014 guideline; applies to adults with persistent, bothersome tinnitus
  • Sample: Guideline update (professional society panel)
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

STRONG: distinguish bothersome from non-bothersome tinnitus; ask about risk of self-harm; do not order neuroimaging solely to evaluate tinnitus unless concerning features are present. RECOMMENDS: targeted history and examination; comprehensive audiologic evaluation; patient education; hearing-aid evaluation for hearing difficulty with persistent bothersome tinnitus; referral for cochlear-implant evaluation when audiologic criteria are met; CBT; documenting outcomes. AGAINST: routine antidepressants, anticonvulsants, anxiolytics or intratympanic medications for a primary tinnitus indication; dietary supplements; rTMS. OPTIONS: ask about somatosensory features; sound enrichment may be recommended; bimodal neuromodulation may be offered.

SOURCE MATERIAL ACTUALLY REVIEWED: the PubMed abstracts of the executive summary (PMID 42861486; doi 10.1002/ohn.70418) and of the full guideline (PMID 42861423), read on 19 October 2026. The full text was not accessible, so the strength of each statement is as listed in the abstract; the evidence profiles, rationale and caveats behind each statement have NOT been read. Guidelines are consensus documents, not new trial data. Published October 2026; the 2014 AAO-HNSF record is kept as the historical guideline.
