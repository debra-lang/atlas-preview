Tinnitus Functional Index (TFI): development and validation

Record details

  • Authors: Meikle MB, et al.
  • Journal / source: Ear & Hearing (2012)
  • Design: Measurement-standard record (questionnaire development/validation)
  • Sample: Development/validation samples
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

The TFI (0–100) measures tinnitus IMPACT/severity across 8 domains and was designed to be responsive to treatment change; a reduction of about 13 points is the commonly used threshold for meaningful improvement. It measures how much tinnitus affects a person — NOT how loud the tinnitus is.

This is the provenance for the ≥13-point 'clinically meaningful' TFI threshold used on this site.
