SPI-1005 (ebselen) Phase 2b in Ménière's disease — ClinicalTrials.gov posted results

Record details

  • Authors: Sound Pharmaceuticals (sponsor)
  • Journal / source: ClinicalTrials.gov posted results (first posted 14 August 2023; no peer-reviewed publication found) (2023)
  • Design: Randomized, quadruple-masked, placebo-controlled Phase 2b trial (8 weeks)
  • Sample: 149 enrolled; 124 analysed (41 placebo / 42 SPI-1005 200 mg / 41 SPI-1005 400 mg twice daily)
  • Primary endpoint: Hearing, Words-in-Noise, TFI, tinnitus loudness (TFI item 2) and vertigo (each listed as primary on the registry)
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: No

What this research found

Tinnitus loudness improved by ≥2 points (TFI item 2) in 14/41 (34%) on placebo, 15/42 (36%) on 200 mg and 14/41 (34%) on 400 mg. TFI improved by ≥10 points in 20/41 (49%), 19/42 (45%) and 13/41 (32%).

No advantage over placebo on loudness or tinnitus impact. Ménière's disease population.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Registry-only reporting; Ménière's disease, not primary tinnitus; loudness is a single TFI item; responder analysis only.

Funding / conflicts: Sponsor-run (Sound Pharmaceuticals).
