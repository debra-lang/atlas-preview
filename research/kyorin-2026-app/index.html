Smartphone tinnitus app: double-blind sham-controlled RCT (Japan)

Record details

  • Authors: Wasano K, Kawasaki T, Goto F, et al.
  • Journal / source: JAMA Otolaryngology–Head & Neck Surgery (2026)
  • Design: Double-blind sham-controlled RCT, 3 centers
  • Sample: 60
  • Primary endpoint: THI at week 16
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: No

What this research found

At week 16: THI −16.8 (active) vs +3.6 (sham — the sham arm slightly worsened, which flatters the difference); between-group −20.4 points (95% CI −28.2 to −12.6); meaningful improvement 70% vs 27%.

Per the authors, the first DOUBLE-BLIND sham-app-controlled RCT of a tinnitus digital therapeutic (earlier app RCTs were single-blind). Registration jRCTs032230359.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

N=60, single country, industry-sponsored.

Funding / conflicts: Sponsored by Kyorin Pharmaceutical; several authors received sponsor fees.
