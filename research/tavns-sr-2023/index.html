Transcutaneous auricular vagus nerve stimulation for tinnitus: systematic review with meta-analysis

Record details

  • Authors: Fernández-Hernando D, et al.
  • Journal / source: Journal of Clinical Medicine (2023)
  • Design: Systematic review with meta-analysis (4 studies pooled)
  • Sample: Small heterogeneous pilots
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

Pooled meta-analysis of 4 studies found a statistically positive effect on tinnitus disability of LOW CLINICAL RELEVANCE; underlying studies small and heterogeneous; evidence insufficient for practice.

A multicenter double-blind RCT of taVNS + notched music (Sun Yat-Sen, NCT05417711) is the first adequately sized test.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

See the record summary above.

Funding / conflicts: Independent.

Corrections to this record

  • 2026-09-04: Corrected: 'narrative synthesis only' was false — the review includes a 4-study meta-analysis with a pooled positive disability effect of low clinical relevance; citation completed (Fernández-Hernando et al., J Clin Med 2023, DOI 10.3390/jcm12113673).
