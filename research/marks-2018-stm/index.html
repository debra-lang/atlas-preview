Auditory-somatosensory bimodal stimulation desynchronizes brain circuitry (first human pilot)

Record details

  • Authors: Marks KL, …, Shore SE.
  • Journal / source: Science Translational Medicine (2018)
  • Design: Randomized crossover pilot
  • Sample: 20
  • Primary endpoint: Loudness and TFI
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

Reduced tinnitus loudness and TFI vs auditory-only; established the timing-dependent plasticity approach in humans.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Pilot size.

Funding / conflicts: NIH-funded; investigator patent interests disclosed.
