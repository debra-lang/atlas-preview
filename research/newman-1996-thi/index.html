Tinnitus Handicap Inventory (THI): development

Record details

  • Authors: Newman CW, et al.
  • Journal / source: Archives of Otolaryngology–Head & Neck Surgery (1996)
  • Design: Measurement-standard record (questionnaire development)
  • Sample: Development sample
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

The THI (0–100) quantifies tinnitus-related handicap in daily life. Like the TFI, it is a DISTRESS/impact measure, not a loudness measure; a ~20-point change (or 7 points in some analyses) is commonly discussed as meaningful, with thresholds varying by study.
