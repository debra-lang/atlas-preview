Kalmeda app (mobile CBT-based therapy): RCT behind Germany's DiGA listing

Record details

  • Authors: Walter U, Pennig S, Kottmann T, et al.
  • Journal / source: PLOS Digital Health (2023)
  • Design: RCT
  • Sample: 187
  • Primary endpoint: Tinnitus Questionnaire
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: No

What this research found

Significant TQ reduction vs control, Cohen d = 1.1 (p<0.001). Kalmeda is permanently listed in the German DiGA registry (#350, permanent since Dec 2021; prescribable, reimbursed) on this basis.

Control was 3-month DELAYED treatment (waitlist-type), which inflates effect sizes vs sham-controlled designs — the d=1.1 is not comparable head-to-head with sham-controlled trials. A 9-month extension RCT was published in JMIR 2025.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Waitlist-type control; no sham app.

Funding / conflicts: Manufacturer-involved.
