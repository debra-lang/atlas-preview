Pharmacotherapy for subjective tinnitus: network meta-analysis of 60 RCTs

Record details

  • Authors: Li P, Che C, Wu Y, et al.
  • Journal / source: BMJ Open (2025)
  • Design: Systematic review + network meta-analysis (>30 drug interventions)
  • Sample: 60 RCTs
  • Primary endpoint: Various
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

No approved drug exists; 78% of included studies had methodological concerns; evidence quality low to very low. Tentative signals (antioxidants, GABA agonists) are hypothesis-generating only and conflict with Cochrane.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Garbage-in problem: weak underlying trials.

Funding / conflicts: Independent.
