Notched music vs ordinary music: independent RCT

Record details

  • Authors: Therdphaothai J, Atipas S, Suvansit K, Prakairungthong S, Thongyai K, Limviriyakul S
  • Journal / source: Journal of International Advanced Otology (2021)
  • Design: Double-blind RCT, 6 months
  • Sample: 104
  • Primary endpoint: THI, VAS
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

No significant THI difference between tailor-made notched music and ordinary music (N=104). The abstract notes a greater score reduction in the treatment group during the follow-up period — a secondary observation that does not alter the negative primary findings.

The Münster trials reported modest effects mainly on perceived loudness in tonal tinnitus.
