Real-world Lenire outcomes: US clinic chart review

Record details

  • Authors: Kasper CA, May JM, Crossland NE, Lim HH
  • Journal / source: American Journal of Audiology (2026)
  • Design: Retrospective single-site chart review
  • Sample: 140 fitted (66 of 104 'bothered' patients analyzed at 12 weeks)
  • Primary endpoint: THI change
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: No

What this research found

Among the 66 bothered-tinnitus patients (THI≥38) with 12-week data, 81.8% (95% CI 70.9–89.3) achieved ≥7-point THI improvement; mean −23.8 points. Non-bothered patients showed essentially no change.

Uncontrolled real-world data with substantial follow-up attrition (66/104) and counseling co-intervention; consistent with trials but cannot separate treatment from placebo/natural course.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Retrospective, uncontrolled, manufacturer-linked, 37% attrition in the analyzed group.

Funding / conflicts: Senior author Hubert Lim is Neuromod's CSO; Neuromod funded chart-review support.
