Betahistine for tinnitus (Cochrane Review)

Record details

  • Authors: Wegner I, et al.
  • Journal / source: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews (2018)
  • Design: Systematic review
  • Sample: 5 studies, N≈305
  • Primary endpoint: Tinnitus loudness and severity
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

'Absence of evidence' of any effect on subjective idiopathic tinnitus vs placebo; well tolerated.
