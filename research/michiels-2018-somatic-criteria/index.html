Diagnostic criteria for somatosensory tinnitus: international Delphi consensus

Record details

  • Authors: Michiels S, et al.
  • Journal / source: Trends in Hearing (2018)
  • Design: Consensus criteria (subtype-identification context record)
  • Sample: International expert Delphi
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

Consensus criteria for identifying the SOMATOSENSORY subtype (tinnitus modulated by jaw/neck movement, associated with somatic dysfunction) — the subtype in which cervical physiotherapy has its evidence. Identifying the right subtype is a clinical task; this record documents that formal criteria exist.
