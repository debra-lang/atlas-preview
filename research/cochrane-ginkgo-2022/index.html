Ginkgo biloba for tinnitus (Cochrane Review)

Record details

  • Authors: Sereda M, et al.
  • Journal / source: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews (2022)
  • Design: Systematic review
  • Sample: 1,915 (12 studies)
  • Primary endpoint: Tinnitus severity
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: Yes · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

Ginkgo 'may have little to no effect' on tinnitus; the evidence is very uncertain.
