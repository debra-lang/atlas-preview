Tinnitus — current developments (state-of-the-field overview)

Record details

  • Authors: Mazurek B, Steinmetzger K, Boecking B, Hesse G, Brueggemann P.
  • Journal / source: HNO (2025)
  • Design: Narrative review
  • Sample: —
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

CBT 'remains the gold standard.' Neuromodulation's clinical effectiveness judged 'relatively low'; for bimodal tongue stimulation, significant effects only in moderate-severe tinnitus and 'proof of clinical relevance is still lacking.'
