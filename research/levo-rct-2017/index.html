Tinnitus-matched sound during sleep (Levo): randomized trial

Record details

  • Authors: Theodoroff SM, et al.
  • Journal / source: American Journal of Audiology (2017)
  • Design: RCT: tinnitus-matched stimulus vs noise masker vs bedside sound generator
  • Sample: 60
  • Primary endpoint: TFI, tinnitus intensity ratings
  • Randomized: Yes · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: No

What this research found

The sound-match arm showed the greatest reduction in rated tinnitus intensity; TFI improved across arms without clear sole superiority of the sound-match arm — small study, no long-term data.

VA Portland site; sponsor-linked.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Small; no long-term follow-up; no independent replication.

Funding / conflicts: Sponsor-linked.

Corrections to this record

  • 2026-09-04: Corrected TFI attribution: sole superiority of the sound-match arm on TFI is not supported; superiority claim confined to rated intensity.
