Sex-specific association of tinnitus with suicide attempts (population-based)

Record details

  • Authors: Lugo A, et al.
  • Journal / source: JAMA Otolaryngology–Head & Neck Surgery (2019)
  • Design: Cross-sectional population study (distress-risk context record, not treatment evidence)
  • Sample: Population-based (Sweden, STAGE cohort)
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

Severe tinnitus was associated with higher odds of reported suicide attempts, most clearly in women. ASSOCIATION, not causation — but it documents that severe tinnitus distress can co-occur with serious psychological crisis, which is why this platform points people with severe distress to urgent professional help.

Cross-sectional design cannot establish direction of causality; comorbid depression/anxiety are major factors.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Cross-sectional; self-reported outcomes.

Funding / conflicts: Independent.
