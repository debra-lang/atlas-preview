Cochrane: antidepressants for tinnitus (CD003853)

Record details

  • Authors: Baldo P, et al.
  • Journal / source: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews (2012)
  • Design: Cochrane systematic review
  • Sample: 6 trials, 610 patients
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

Verbatim conclusion: 'There is as yet insufficient evidence to say that antidepressant drug therapy improves tinnitus.'

Distinct from treating comorbid depression, which can itself be worthwhile.

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Trial quality low; heterogeneous drugs.

Funding / conflicts: Independent.
