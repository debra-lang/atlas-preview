COMiT'ID: core outcome domains for tinnitus treatment trials

Record details

  • Authors: Hall DA, et al.
  • Journal / source: Trends in Hearing (2018)
  • Design: Measurement-standard record (core outcome set consensus)
  • Sample: International consensus (Delphi + consensus meetings)
  • Primary endpoint: —
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: Yes

What this research found

International consensus on WHICH outcome domains tinnitus trials should measure, by intervention type — formalizing that different treatments target different outcomes and trials must not be compared on a single universal 'effectiveness' number. This is the methodological basis for our refusal to show one effectiveness score.
