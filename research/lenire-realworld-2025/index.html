First US real-world Lenire cohort (Communications Medicine)

Record details

  • Authors: McMahan E, Lim HH, et al.
  • Journal / source: Communications Medicine (Nature Portfolio) (2025)
  • Design: Retrospective real-world cohort
  • Sample: 212 fitted / 220 enrolled
  • Primary endpoint: THI change
  • Randomized: No · Sham/placebo-controlled: No · Independent of the manufacturer: No

What this research found

91.5% responder rate; mean THI improvement 27.8 points — open-label, uncontrolled real-world data.

First of two US chart reviews (the second is Kasper 2026, Am J Audiol).

Limitations and conflicts of interest

Uncontrolled; retrospective.

Funding / conflicts: Manufacturer-linked.
