# Independent scientific analysis of the Tinnitus Evidence database (2026-09-05)

Full text delivered in session 2026-09-05. Summary of findings:

CAVEATS: pattern analysis of the curated 73-record DB (selection effects; small cells; cross-study reasoning,
not meta-analysis). Hypothesis-generating only; not clinical guidance.

PATTERNS
1. Loudness measurement gap: only 18/51 randomized records measured any percept-level outcome; the field's
   "distress-only" conclusion is partly circular. NICE discourages matching, worsening it.
2. Nonspecific response ~10-15 THI/TFI points in nearly all control/active arms (gabapentin 11.0 placebo;
   MOST all-arms ~15 with 4.4 spread; TRT=standard care; sham rTMS=real). Kyorin beat a MATCHED sham app by
   20.4 -> specific effects are detectable when engagement is controlled. Proposal: publish a pooled
   "expected nonspecific improvement" reference curve.
3. SOMATOSENSORY CONVERGENCE (main finding): the only sham-controlled psychoacoustic loudness win (Shore,
   -10.9 dB wk12) came from the only trial that REQUIRED somatic tinnitus. Independent supporting signals:
   68-80% somatic modulation prevalence; Michiels PT; Eladl; Tullberg TMJ 43% vs 12% at 2 yrs (durable);
   Michiels 2018 criteria; DCN mechanism. Pure-auditory manipulations keep failing replication (Stein,
   Atipas, MOST HFEM worse than plain music).
4. Only structural-cause removal is durable (stenting 88.6%, SSWR 83.8% at 5y, TMJ 2y); central effects fade
   (rTMS 6-mo dispute; Michiels 53%->24%). GAP: zero maintenance/re-dosing designs among 21 tracked trials.
5. Drug graveyard is bottom-up (AM-101, OTO-313, neramexane, AUT00063, gabapentin, FX-322); survivors are
   central/multimodal. Anti-"too late" caveat: TACTT was acute (<=3 mo) and still failed.

HYPOTHESIS: somatic-modulation capacity as a response biomarker for bimodal/somatosensory interventions
(DCN pathway). Support: enrollment-on-variable produced the best loudness result; no bimodal trial ever
stratified on it; dilution would explain Lenire's distress-only broad-population profile. Contradictions:
same-group replication of both device programs; Shore ITT wk6 p=.08; fade-out of somatic PT; capacity may
be too prevalent to discriminate. Tests: (1) add 5-minute modulation battery + prespecified interaction to
ongoing trials (NCT05434637, NCT06895824, NCT05417711); (2) TENT/Michigan dataset reanalysis; (3) 2x2 RCT
bimodal vs sound-only x modulators vs non-modulators with loudness-matching co-primary. Null interaction
falsifies. Certainty: plausible + cheap to test, NOT likely-true.

SECONDARY: every trial should carry one percept-level outcome; Kyorin-style matched-engagement controls
should replace waitlists as the minimum comparator.

## CORRECTION (2026-09-05, same day — after adversarial novelty audit; see novelty-audit-2026-09-05.md)
The statement "the field has inexplicably never run it" was WRONG. The binary form of the hypothesis has
been directly tested at least three times (van der Wal 2020 PMID 33041758; Spencer 2022 PMID 36090280;
Lee 2020 PMID 32784160) — all null — and proposed since Levine 2007. The hypothesis is retained only in a
modified, graded form (number/direction/consistency of modulation), which has post-hoc support (Shore 2023
eFigure 7; Rocha & Sanchez 2012) and no adequately powered prespecified test. See the novelty audit for the
corrected wording and the TENT-A1 unpublished-subgroup finding.
