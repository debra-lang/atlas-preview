# Phase 18 Holdout List — LOCKED before inventory read (2026-09-04)

All items verified via Europe PMC REST / web search. Constructed independently; inventories NOT read at time of writing.

## (a) RCTs / meta-analyses 2000-2020
1. Tinnitus Retraining Therapy Trial (TRTT) — Scherer RW / Formby C (TRTT Research Group), 2019, PMID 31120533, JAMA Otolaryngol — largest RCT of TRT (n=151); TRT not superior to standard care; reshaped view of TRT.
2. Specialised CBT-based stepped care vs usual care — Cima RFF, 2012, PMID 22633033, Lancet — n=492 RCT underpinning CBT-based multidisciplinary care and EU practice.
3. rTMS for chronic tinnitus, VA RCT — Folmer RL, 2015, PMID 26181507, JAMA Otolaryngol — positive US rTMS trial, key counterpoint in the rTMS debate.
4. Cochrane: CBT for tinnitus — Fuller T, 2020, PMID 31912887, CD012614 — definitive synthesis: CBT improves QoL, gold-standard evidence.
5. Cochrane: Sound therapy (amplification/sound generators) — Sereda M, 2018, PMID 30589445, CD013094 — key null/uncertain evidence for the most-used intervention class.

## (b) Important items 2021-2026
6. TENT-A2 bimodal neuromodulation RCT — Conlon B, 2022, PMID 35773272, Sci Rep — n=191; parameter-varied Lenire trial, basis of device momentum.
7. Auricle bi-sensory stimulation phase 2 RCT — Jones GR (Shore SE lab), 2023, PMID 37266943, JAMA Netw Open — personalized auditory-somatosensory stimulation for somatic tinnitus.
8. Global prevalence & incidence meta-analysis — Jarach CM, 2022, PMID 35939312, JAMA Neurol — 14.4% prevalence; the standard burden citation.
9. UNITI RCT: single vs combination treatment — Schoisswohl S, 2025, PMID 41271763, Nat Commun — largest EU multicenter tinnitus RCT (CBT/HA/sound/counseling combos).
10. Long-term efficacy of audiologist-guided internet CBT (US) — Beukes EW, 2022, PMID 36353148, Internet Interv — telehealth CBT durability, access-relevant.

## (c) Negative / failed treatments
11. AM-101 / Keyzilen (intratympanic esketamine) — van de Heyning P, 2014, PMID 24603353, Otol Neurotol; phase 3 TACTT2 (NCT01803646) / TACTT3 failed — flagship failed drug program.
12. Neramexane — Suckfüll M, 2011, PMID 21223542, BMC Ear Nose Throat Disord — NMDA/nicotinic antagonist; phase 3 program failed.
13. AUT00063 (Kv3 modulator), QUIET-1 phase 2a — Hall DA, 2019, PMID 30939361, Hear Res — negative; terminated early for futility.
14. Ginkgo biloba, large double-blind RCT — Drew S & Davies E, 2001, PMID 11154618, BMJ — n=1121, negative; anchors the anti-supplement evidence.
15. 1-Hz rTMS sham-controlled multicenter trial — Landgrebe M, 2017, PMID 28807845, Brain Stimul — n=146, negative; largest rigorous rTMS trial.

## (d) Guideline / regulatory events
16. AAO-HNS Clinical Practice Guideline: Tinnitus — Tunkel DE, 2014, PMID 25273878, Otolaryngol Head Neck Surg — US standard of care; recommends against drugs/supplements.
17. NICE NG155 Tinnitus: assessment and management — 11 March 2020, nice.org.uk/guidance/ng155 — UK care pathway incl. referral criteria.
18. European multidisciplinary guideline — Cima RFF, 2019, PMID 30847513, HNO — pan-EU treatment algorithm (CBT strongly recommended).
19. FDA De Novo grant for Lenire — 7 March 2023 (Neuromod Devices; TENT-A3 pivotal) — first bimodal neuromodulation device authorized for tinnitus in US.

## (e) Pulsatile / somatic subtype
20. Somatosensory tinnitus diagnostic criteria (Delphi consensus) — Michiels S, 2018, PMID 30213235, Trends Hear — how to identify the somatic subtype.
21. Sigmoid sinus wall reconstruction for pulsatile tinnitus — Eisenman DJ, 2011, PMID 21799456, Otol Neurotol — standardized surgical cure for a treatable PT cause.
22. Pulsatile tinnitus: imaging and differential diagnosis — Hofmann E, 2013, PMID 23885280, Dtsch Arztebl Int — canonical workup reference (dural AVF, IIH etc. red flags).

## (f) Wildcards (depth probes)
23. Tinnitus Functional Index development/validation — Meikle MB, 2012, PMID 22156949, Ear Hear — the field's primary responsiveness-validated outcome measure.
24. COMiT'ID core outcome domain sets — Hall DA, 2018, PMID 30488765, Trends Hear — international core outcome standards for tinnitus trials.
25. Sex-specific association of tinnitus with suicide attempts — Lugo A, 2019, PMID 31046059, JAMA Otolaryngol — safety/severity signal; patient-facing platforms must handle distress risk.
