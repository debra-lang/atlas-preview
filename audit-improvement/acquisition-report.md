# Evidence acquisition report (research agent, 2026-09-04)
Provenance for the Phase 5/6/13 database additions. Rules applied: nothing guessed; UNVERIFIED marked where a field could not be confirmed from a source actually read. THI/TFI/TQ = distress/severity outcomes, not loudness.

## P1. "DFCRS trial" = MOST trial (DFCRS is the experimental ARM name)
Tang D, Gu D, Gong J, Liu G, Zhou L, Dai A, Huo Y, Guan P, Zhang J, Huang X, Wang Y, Sun S, Li H. "Efficacy and safety of a modified sound therapy for patients with subjective tinnitus (MOST): a multicentre, double-blind, randomised controlled trial." eClinicalMedicine 2025;90:103671 (online 2025-12-08). DOI 10.1016/j.eclinm.2025.103671 · PMID 41497514 · PMCID PMC12766481 · ChiCTR2000039007.
N=440 randomized (UM 111 / UM+NBN 110 / HFEM 108 / DFCRS 111), all in ITT; three Shanghai academic hospitals; daily 2-h listening, 9 months; participants and clinicians blinded; NO no-treatment/sham arm (UM = unmodified music reference).
Arms: UM unmodified music · UM+NBN music + 1/3-oct narrowband noise at tinnitus pitch · HFEM fixed 6–9 kHz +10 dB enhancement · DFCRS dynamic two-band modulation around the tinnitus pitch.
Primary endpoint (paper-defined, two-part): (a) complete remission = THI 0 at any visit within 9 months; (b) otherwise THI change baseline→9 months. Result: 1 participant total reached remission (HFEM arm). THI overall: median 50.00 [36–62] → 35.00 [24–48], p<0.0001. GEE vs UM: DFCRS −4.37 [−6.25, −2.48] p<0.0001 (better); HFEM +2.11 [0.11, 4.11] p=0.039 (WORSE than UM); UM+NBN +0.31 [−1.61, 2.23] p=0.75. No multiplicity adjustment (authors' stated limitation).
Loudness: VAS-loudness baseline 5.00 [4–6], reduced in ALL arms; DFCRS greater reductions vs other arms in first 3 months (F(5,236)=16.02, p<0.0001). VAS-annoyance F(5,323)=21.12 p<0.0001 favoring DFCRS. HADS-A/D, AIS-8 interactions favored DFCRS. 3-month posttreatment: effects sustained; slight/mild THI at final follow-up DFCRS 71.01% vs UM 60.0%.
Safety: "No adverse events were reported in any group." Funding: Chinese public agencies; no competing interests declared.
Authors: design cannot "unequivocally distinguish [benefits] from placebo effects or natural symptom fluctuations."
Precursor (non-randomized): Tang D et al., J Med Internet Res 2025, DOI 10.2196/60150, PMID 39823588. No secondary analyses of MOST found.
Sources read: Europe PMC search; efetch PMID 41497514; Europe PMC PMC12766481 fullTextXML.

## P2.2 Cima 2012 (Lancet)
Cima RFF, Maes IH, Joore MA, Scheyen DJWM, El Refaie A, Baguley DM, Anteunis LJC, van Breukelen GJP, Vlaeyen JWS. Lancet 2012;379(9830):1951–59. DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(12)60469-3 · PMID 22633033 · NCT00733044. N=492 randomized of 741 screened (245 specialised / 247 usual care); patients and assessors masked. Primary outcomes over 12 months: HUI +0.059 [0.025, 0.094] d=0.24 p=0.0009; TQ −8.062 [−10.829, −5.295] d=0.43 p<0.0001; THI −7.506 [−10.661, −4.352] d=0.45 p<0.0001. Effective across severity strata. No adverse events. Funding ZonMW (NL). Limitations beyond abstract: UNVERIFIED.

## P2.3 AAO-HNSF CPG Tinnitus (Tunkel 2014)
Otolaryngol HNS 2014;151(2 Suppl):S1–S40. DOI 10.1177/0194599814545325 · PMID 25273878 (exec summary PMID 25274374). Read from abstracts: hearing-aid evaluation RECOMMENDED (with hearing loss); CBT RECOMMENDED; sound therapy = OPTION ("may recommend"); medical therapy (antidepressants/anticonvulsants/anxiolytics/intratympanic) AGAINST routine use; dietary supplements (Ginkgo, melatonin, zinc) AGAINST; rTMS AGAINST routine use; acupuncture NO RECOMMENDATION. Full-text verbatim statement wording beyond this: UNVERIFIED.

## P2.4 NICE NG155 (2020) — from official NICE pathway PDF (NBK557037 mirror)
Amplification: "Offer" with hearing loss affecting communication; "Consider" with hearing loss without communication difficulty; "Do not offer" without hearing loss. Sound therapy: "We were unable to make recommendations for practice in this area." Psychological therapies stepped: (1) digital tinnitus-related CBT (psychologists), (2) group psychological interventions (MBCT/ACT/CBT), (3) individual CBT. Betahistine: "Do not offer betahistine to treat tinnitus." Neuromodulation: no practice recommendation. Psychoacoustic pitch/loudness matching for assessment: do not offer. Other drug do-not-offers: in full guideline per Langguth 2023, exact wording UNVERIFIED.

## P2.5 European multidisciplinary guideline (Cima 2019, HNO)
HNO 2019;67(Suppl 1):10–42. DOI 10.1007/s00106-019-0633-7 · PMID 30847513. Stances verified via SECONDARY source (Langguth et al., J Clin Med 2023, PMC10178961; primary full text inaccessible): CBT strong FOR; sound therapy recommendation-for-RESEARCH only; hearing aids FOR with hearing loss; pharmacotherapy weak AGAINST; tES neuromodulation AGAINST. Verbatim wording/grading UNVERIFIED.
Guideline disagreement snapshot (per Langguth 2023): sound therapy — AAO-HNSF option / NICE & EU research-only / German S3 (Mazurek 2021) AGAINST sound generators. CBT: all for. Drugs: all against. rTMS: against or no recommendation.

## P2.6 Hearing-aid amplification
(a) Hoare 2014 Cochrane CD010151 (with Edmondson-Jones, Sereda, Akeroyd, Hall). DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD010151.pub2 · PMID 24482186. ONE RCT, 91 participants (HA vs sound generators). Verbatim conclusion: "…there is currently no evidence to support or refute their use as a more routine intervention for tinnitus."
(b) "JSLHR 2023 HA RCT": NOT FOUND — zero hits; do not cite. Closest verified evidence: CD010151 + Sereda 2018 CD013094 (8 studies/590, low quality, no superiority evidence).

## P2.7 Sigmoid sinus wall reconstruction (SSWR), pulsatile tinnitus
SR: Sathya A et al., Interv Neuroradiol, DOI 10.1177/15910199241231325, PMID 38515373: surgical complete resolution 77.6% (83/107 diverticula, 105 pts), incomplete 11.2%, significant complications 9.3%; endovascular arm: 27/27 complete/near-complete, 3.7% complications.
Long-term cohort: Ahanotu AE et al., Otol Neurotol 2024, DOI 10.1097/MAO.0000000000004352, PMID 39439073: 35 pts/37 ears; short-term success 97.3%; 5-yr 83.8%; recurrence 13.5%; IIH diagnosed 8.6%.
Critical review: Mamikoglu B, Audiology Research, DOI 10.3390/audiolres16040106, PMID 42645117: stricter criteria → 64.9% elimination in one cohort; complications incl. IIH 3/35, papilledema 5/35, stenting needed 2/35.
No RCTs. Recurrence predictors: Bai X (PMID 41997844, N=93); Dai C (PMID 41138317, N=51, recurrence 31.4%).

## P3 (verified key numbers)
8. Folmer 2015 rTMS: JAMA Otolaryngol 2015;141(8):716–22, DOI 10.1001/jamaoto.2015.1219, PMID 26181507, NCT01104207. 70 randomized, 64 analyzed (32/arm); 1-Hz rTMS ×10 workdays vs placebo coil; primary TFI responder status; responders 18/32 (56%) vs 7/32 (22%), p<.005. (Distress outcome.)
9. Landgrebe 2017 rTMS: Brain Stimul, DOI 10.1016/j.brs.2017.08.001, PMID 28807845, ISRCTN89848288. N=163 randomized (75 real/78 sham) — NOT ≈146. Primary TQ change day 12: −0.5 vs +0.5; adj diff −1.0 [−3.2, 1.2], p=0.36. NEGATIVE.
10. Michiels 2016: Man Ther 2016;26:125–131, DOI 10.1016/j.math.2016.08.005, PMID 27592038. N=38 (19/19 immediate vs delayed), 6 wks cervical PT. TFI p=0.04; NBQ p<0.001; 53% substantial improvement post-treatment, maintained in only 24% at 6-week follow-up.
11. FX-322 Ph2b (2023-02-13, company/news): FX-322-208 N=142 failed primary (speech perception day 90) and all secondaries; program discontinued; pivot to MS. NO tinnitus outcome found in any FX-322 publication read (Ph1b McLean, Otol Neurotol 2021, PMID 33617194 — no tinnitus measures). Tinnitus-measured claims: UNVERIFIED.
12. Baldo 2012 Cochrane CD003853, DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD003853.pub3, PMID 22972065. 6 trials/610. Verbatim: "There is as yet insufficient evidence to say that antidepressant drug therapy improves tinnitus."
13. MindEar pilot RCT: Bardy F et al., Frontiers in Audiology and Otology 2024, DOI 10.3389/fauot.2023.1302215. N=30 randomized/28 analyzed; Tinnibot alone vs + 4 psychologist video sessions; TFI decrease 11.24 vs 19.99 post-treatment; clinically significant 42% vs 64%, both 64% at 16 wks; between-group NS. No registration stated; PMID not found. COI: first author co-founded Odio Tech. (Adjacent 2026: Jackson et al., N=105, Internet Interventions, PMID 42023129.)
14. Neramexane: Ph2 Suckfüll 2011, BMC ENT Disord, DOI 10.1186/1472-6815-11-1, PMID 21223542, NCT00405886, N=431 — primary THI-12 at wk 16 NOT significant. Ph3 registry-verified: NCT00739635 (411), NCT00772980 (400), NCT00955799 (455) completed 2010–11; extension NCT00827008 terminated Dec 2011; Ph3 efficacy NEVER PUBLISHED — "failure" per se UNVERIFIED; verified: completed, unpublished, program abandoned.
15. Hurtuk 2011 melatonin: Ann Otol Rhinol Laryngol 2011;120(7):433–40, DOI 10.1177/000348941112000703, PMID 21859051. Randomized DB crossover N=61, 3 mg nightly; significant decreases in tinnitus matching + self-rated tinnitus vs placebo (p<0.05); better sleep.
16. Okamoto 2010: PNAS 107(3):1207–10, DOI 10.1073/pnas.0911268107, PMID 20080545. n=8 target-notched vs n=8 placebo-notched, 12 months: significantly reduced subjective tinnitus LOUDNESS + reduced cortical activity. (Loudness outcome; tiny.)
17. Stein 2016: BMC Neurol 16:38, DOI 10.1186/s12883-016-0558-7, PMID 26987755, ISRCTN04840953. DB RCT N=100 notched music 3 mo: primary outcomes (THQ + VAS) NO significant effect; late secondary loudness reduction at follow-up.
18. Davis 2007 Neuromonics: Ear Hear 28(2):242–259, DOI 10.1097/aud.0b013e3180312619, PMID 17496674. N=35, randomized to TWO VARIANTS of Neuromonics (no untreated control); TRQ ≥40% improvement in 91% at 6 mo. Registration/COI details UNVERIFIED.
19. Cheung 2019 caudate DBS Ph1: J Neurosurg, DOI 10.3171/2019.4.JNS19347, PMID 31553940, NCT01988688. 6 implanted/5 completed; TFI −23.3 (SE 12.4), THI −30.8; responders 3/5 TFI, 4/5 THI; unblinded N=5.
20. Gabapentin: Piccirillo 2007, Arch Otolaryngol HNS, DOI 10.1001/archotol.133.4.390, PMID 17438255. 8-wk DB RCT, 115 analyzed (59 vs 56): THI change 11.3 vs 11.0, diff 0.03 [−5.5, 6.2], p=.91 — negative. Hoekstra 2011 Cochrane CD007960, PMID 21735419, 7 RCTs/453: no large positive effect; small effect "of doubtful clinical significance."
21. Cilcare CIL001: COMPANY-REPORTED ONLY (cilcare.com, trade press): cochlear-synaptopathy target, intratympanic, Phase 1b/2a program advancing to Phase 2a in chronic tinnitus (US, MGH) 2025–26; €40M Series A Dec 2024. No peer-reviewed efficacy data; efficacy claims UNVERIFIED.
22. Jüris 2014 CBT-for-hyperacusis RCT: Behav Res Ther 54:30–7, DOI 10.1016/j.brat.2014.01.001, PMID 24508581. N=60 CBT vs waitlist; moderate-to-large effects (d≈0.67–0.69 on LDLs), maintained 12 mo. (Adjacent evidence.)
