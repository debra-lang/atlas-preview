# Screening/examination reconstruction (2026-09-04) — basis for public comprehensiveness claims

Reconstructed from engine logs (known_ids, audit-log.jsonl, engine-status git history), audit ledgers
(audit-baseline-v1/), and documented agent reports (acquisition, re-verification, holdout). Unlogged activity
counted conservatively or not at all. No promotional rounding.

| Level | Number | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Search results screened (title/metadata, duplicates incl.) | floor ~400; realistically 500–1,000 | 109 logged scanner examinations + 63 benchmark + 33 precision set + 64 identifier resolutions + 25 holdout + ~150 documented agent search/fetch calls, many returning 10–100-title result lists (list impressions not individually countable) |
| Unique records examined (deduplicated) | ~170 (150–200) | 67 included + ~45 scanner candidates + 21 registry + 13 regulatory + ~15 agent-examined-excluded + ~7 holdout-unique + 1 benchmark-unique |
| Examined in detail (methods/results checked vs source) | ~85–90 | all 67 included (64 verified to the number against abstracts/full text/registry) + ~20 context/excluded at results level |
| Included | 67 research records + 21 registry trial records + 13 verified regulatory statuses | 19 SR/MA (7 Cochrane) · 36 RCT/controlled · 3 guidelines · 2 phase trials · 7 other (labeled) |
| Excluded after review | ~100 unique | tinnitus incidental/not treatment evidence (largest); unverifiable/company-only; duplicates of included trials; adjacent-condition; below record threshold. (34 scanner items PENDING evaluation — not excluded) |

Date range: searches unbounded backward (items from 2001 examined); included records span 2007–2026; landmarks
deliberately included (Davis 2007, Okamoto 2010, Cima 2012, guidelines 2014/2019/2020). Weekly automation scans
7-day windows only.

NOT searched (disclose on methodology page): EMBASE, Scopus, Web of Science, PsycINFO, CINAHL; Cochrane CENTRAL
directly; ChiCTR/WHO ICTRP/ISRCTN/EU-CTR directly (no stable public APIs — MOST-trial-class ChiCTR blind spot
documented); non-English literature directly; conference abstracts/preprints systematically.

## Approved public wording
"Several hundred search results across PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, Cochrane, FDA databases and guideline bodies
were screened; around 170 unique studies, trials, guidelines and regulatory records were individually examined;
and every one of the 67 included research records was verified against its primary source."

Also truthful (shorter): "Hundreds of tinnitus research publications and clinical records were screened to
identify the evidence included in Tinnitus Evidence."

NOT defensible: "thousands screened" · "hundreds examined in detail" · "all published tinnitus research" ·
"comprehensive" (unqualified).
